# Synthetic extension figure captions

## ten_seed_performance

Performance across data seeds 100-109. Each realization contains 10,000 rows and 20 features, with 500 designated positives. The shared stratified split gives 6,000 training rows (300 positives) and 4,000 test rows (200 positives); no preprocessing is applied. The left panels vary the one-feature shift magnitude at normal within-block correlation 0.8. The right panels vary normal within-block correlation while positive features remain independent. Delta 0 and rho 0 are separate family-specific null controls without a deliberately introduced signal. Small dots show every seed, large markers show means, and bars show +/-1 sample SD across ten seeds, not confidence intervals. Both metrics have range 0-1; plotting margins retain all SD endpoints even if they slightly exceed that range. Dashed reference lines indicate random-ranking ROC AUC 0.5 and AP prevalence 0.05; finite-sample null AP is not required to equal 0.05. Horizontal offsets separate methods and seeds only for visibility; tick labels identify the actual parameter levels. Lines connect the method means as a guide to the eye, not as estimates at untested strengths. Coincident values, including perfect rankings, are retained. Batch COPOD uses training plus test features for its reference; fixed COPOD and Ledoit-Wolf use training features only. The covariance comparator was chosen for these Gaussian mechanisms, not as a universally superior detector.

## ten_seed_method_differences

Paired method comparisons across all seven conditions and data seeds 100-109. Left panels show fixed-reference COPOD minus batch COPOD; right panels show Ledoit-Wolf squared Mahalanobis distance minus fixed-reference COPOD. Positive differences favour the first method in each title. Each small dot is a difference computed on the same seed, condition and test rows. Large markers and bars show the mean and +/-1 sample SD of those ten differences; the bars are not confidence intervals. The two comparison columns use different horizontal scales to keep the smaller scoring-protocol differences visible. All points and SD endpoints are included. Dashed vertical lines mark zero difference. Vertical jitter only separates seeds. The family divider separates single-feature shift settings from dependence settings; the controls are not pooled. Ledoit-Wolf minus fixed-reference COPOD at rho 0.8 is the prespecified principal relational comparison. These are descriptive comparisons, without significance or equivalence tests.

## ten_seed_strength_differences

Paired changes from each family's own null control. For each method and seed, the left panels subtract the delta-0 metric from the corresponding delta-2, delta-4 or delta-6 metric. The right panels subtract the rho-0 metric from the corresponding rho-0.4 or rho-0.8 metric. Conditions within a family share the planned latent draws, row identities and split. The two families have different draws and their controls remain separate. Every seed difference is shown; large markers and bars show the mean and +/-1 sample SD of the ten paired differences, not confidence intervals. Negative values are retained. Dashed lines indicate zero difference. Axes include every seed and SD endpoint, with the same vertical scale across families for a given metric. Horizontal offsets only separate methods and seeds. Connecting lines guide the eye between tested levels. A larger single-feature shift also changes positive-group correlations; these results should not be interpreted as an intervention on individual distributions alone.
